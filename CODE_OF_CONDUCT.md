# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

We are committed to maintaining a harassment-free, respectful environment for everyone who participates in this project — regardless of experience level, background, or identity. All contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to interact with honesty, patience, and professionalism.

## Enforcement

Violations of this code of conduct may be reported privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channels. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
