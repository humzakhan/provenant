## Where Provenant Fits in v0

Provenant is a Go provenance enforcement layer for regulated finance. It is not a SaaS product, agent, database, model, logger, or workflow engine. The library is the core product; CLI, MCP, and HTTP are access surfaces around the same kernel.

### Fits now

- **A Go host embedding the kernel (Mode A).** Mode A is the required deployment shape for hard, non-bypassable regulated output. The Provenant harness is the agent loop; `submit_answer` is the only exit. Use it when unsigned host-channel text is unacceptable in your regulatory context.
- **An MCP or HTTP host (Mode B)** that understands host-channel text is not attested without verification. Mode B gives an architecture contract the host must honor; Provenant cannot enforce it unilaterally across an untrusted host channel.
- **Single-tenant deployments.** Every token, tool call, audit entry, and run carries a `tenant_id`. Cross-tenant token references are rejected at verification. v0 ships single-tenant; multi-tenant packaging is deferred.
- **Regulated-finance numeric-provenance needs.** Specifically: token-level grounding with verifiable lineage back to captured reads, hash-chained audit bundles that are verifiable and replayable offline, and four independent determinism and faithfulness metrics.

### Does NOT fit yet

The following are explicitly deferred in v0. Full detail and rationale is in `docs/KNOWN_TRADE_OFFS.md`.

- Non-Go embedding — v0 embedded path is Go-only. <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />
- Multi-tenant SaaS packaging. <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />
- Hosted skill registry and skill-pack signing beyond local digest status. <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />
- Fuzzy or AI-based matching. <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />
- A complete end-user reconciliation product (with inference, UI, or fuzzy matching). <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />
- LangChain and OpenAI adapters — Claude has been verified end-to-end; other adapters are deferred. <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />
- Streaming and split-turn parallel runs. <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />

## Before You Rely on Output

Work through this checklist before treating any Provenant output as an official regulated conclusion.

### 1. Choose the mode for your risk level

**Mode A is required where unsigned host-channel text is unacceptable.** In Mode A, the substrate owns the loop and `submit_answer` is architecturally non-bypassable. In Mode B (MCP or HTTP), Provenant validates tool inputs, mints tokens, and writes the audit chain — but cannot prevent the host from emitting text on its own channel without going through `submit_answer`. See [Modes](/concepts/modes/) for the full boundary.

### 2. Verify the audit bundle offline

Run both verification steps against the bundle produced by the run:

- **`verify`** — calls `bundle.Verify`, which checks format, audit-chain hash linkage, and sequence continuity across every entry. A passing result confirms the bundle bytes have not been internally altered since production.
- **`replay`** — calls `bundle.Replay`, which re-derives every token from the captured reads in the bundle. Replay is fully offline; it does not re-contact external sources. Note: replay re-derives from captured bytes only — it does not guarantee that two fresh runs on different deployments produce identical chains (see [Replay is offline and captured-deterministic](#replay-is-offline-and-captured-deterministic--not-cross-deployment-equivalent) below).

See [Review Audit Evidence](/guides/review-audit-evidence/) for the full step-by-step procedure.

### 3. Confirm a regulated conclusion is properly attested

A regulated conclusion is **official only when**:

1. It comes from a passing `submit_answer` call, AND
2. The `[PROVENANT-ATTESTED v1 ...]` marker is verified against the audit chain or audit pack from the run bundle.

<CaveatCallout title="Marker text alone is not proof" tone="danger">
  A `[PROVENANT-ATTESTED ...]` marker appearing on host-channel text is NOT by itself proof of attestation — marker text alone is not sufficient. Do NOT treat Mode B host-channel text as attested without the verification steps above. Untested hosts can complete a run as `host_text_only`. Verification requires the bundle bytes.
</CaveatCallout>

### 4. Understand what `bundle.Verify` does and does not prove

<CaveatCallout title="Verify checks integrity, not business correctness" tone="warn">
  `bundle.Verify` checks hash-chain linkage and bundle integrity. It does NOT prove that the right business rule was applied, or that the right source data was used. Correctness is a separately measured metric and is not guaranteed by integrity verification alone.
</CaveatCallout>

## Operational Caveats

### Audit evidence is internally tamper-evident, not externally tamper-proof

<CaveatCallout title="Internally tamper-evident, not externally tamper-proof or notarized" tone="warn">
  v0 audit evidence is internally tamper-evident through hash-chain verification. Provenant can detect internal edits to bundle or audit bytes. It does NOT provide external notarization, third-party witnessing, or tamper-proof guarantees. A maliciously root-compromised host (a root-equivalent actor with direct write access to the database or bundle storage) can construct a new valid chain from scratch; the chain detects edits, it does not prevent a root actor. Do not describe v0 evidence as externally attested or tamper-proof.
</CaveatCallout>

If you need stronger guarantees, the documented operational mitigation is to **replicate audit logs to a separate write-once store and have a separate team run periodic chain verification**. External notarization is <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />.

### Host conformance is self-declared in v0

<CaveatCallout title="Host conformance is self-declared in v0" tone="warn">
  v0 provides no operator-signed attestation and no Provenant-side verification of host conformance. Untested hosts can complete a run as `host_text_only`. Do not rely on host conformance claims that have not been independently verified. Host conformance verification is <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />.
</CaveatCallout>

### Replay is offline and captured-deterministic — not cross-deployment-equivalent

`bundle.Replay` re-derives tokens from captured bytes stored in the bundle. It does not guarantee that two fresh runs on different deployments produce identical chains — external source versions (for example, sanctions lists or FX rates) may differ between deployments. Cross-deployment fresh-run equivalence is not guaranteed.

### Determinism does not imply accuracy

<CaveatCallout title="Determinism ≠ accuracy — use all four metrics" tone="warn">
  Provenant reports four independent metrics: trajectory determinism, decision determinism, faithfulness, and accuracy. These are measured and reported separately. A deterministic system can be reliably wrong. Correctness — whether the right rule was applied, whether the right source data was used — is a separately measured metric. Do not substitute determinism for correctness. Use the four metrics together.
</CaveatCallout>

### Examples are proof fixtures, not a complete product

The reconciliation fixture and derivation-graph demo prove the provenance path works. They are intentionally small demonstrations. They do not infer columns, perform fuzzy matching, read remote files, expose a UI, or run LLM reasoning. They are not a complete reconciliation or derivation product.

## Not Legal Advice

<CaveatCallout title="Regulatory material is context, not legal advice or regulatory approval" tone="warn">
  Provenant's audit and evidence capabilities are **enabled, not certified**. Documentation references to regulatory frameworks — SR 11-7, FINRA 2026 GenAI guidance, NYDFS RAISE Act, MiFID II, and similar — are context, not legal advice or regulatory approval of any deployment. Customers achieve compliance by combining Provenant's evidence with their own governance, legal counsel, and internal model-risk processes. Provenant does not certify, approve, or guarantee that any deployment meets the requirements of any regulatory framework. Regulatory interpretation is <StatusBadge status="Deferred" />.
</CaveatCallout>
